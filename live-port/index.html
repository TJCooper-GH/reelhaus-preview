HOW IT WORKS

From the idea to the final cut.

Six steps, in the order a film gets made. You call the shots at each one.

01 · ANGLE

Start with what’s already working.

Pick a topic people are watching right now, before you write a word.

How Reelhaus helps: Finder shows outlier videos beating their channel’s normal, and Why it won shows what made them work.

02 · SCRIPT

Get a script you can actually shoot.

Every line is a shot you can film, timed from the hook to the payoff.

How Reelhaus helps: The script writer builds the beat sheet from the structure of videos that already work.

03 · SHOTS

Put every shot on the board.

See the whole video as shots and reorder them before anything renders.

How Reelhaus helps: The storyboard turns each line into a shot card with the framing and camera move written for you. Drag a card to reorder.

04 · CAST & LOOK

Same face. Same look. Every shot.

Nobody drifts between takes, and every shot keeps the same film look.

How Reelhaus helps: A character sheet locks each cast member, and a locked look keeps one style across the video.

05 · RENDER

See the price before you render.

Stop burning credits on rerolls. Check it on a still, then render the take you want.

How Reelhaus helps: Each paid step shows its price before it runs, on your own key with zero markup or on credits. If a run fails on Reelhaus’s side, the credits come back.

06 · FIX

Say what to change.

“Tighten the pauses. Swap shot four.” Approve a limit, then let the editor make the change.

How Reelhaus helps: The editor is an AI agent on your timeline. Tell it what to change, and nothing is charged until you press Go.
