HOW IT WORKS

From the idea to the final cut.

Six steps, in the order a film gets made. You call the shots at each one.

01 · ANGLE

Start with what’s already working.

Pick a topic people are watching right now, before you write a word.

How Reelhaus helps: Finder shows outlier videos beating their channel’s normal, and Breakdown shows why they worked.

02 · SCRIPT

Get a script you can actually shoot.

Every line is a shot you can film, timed from the hook to the payoff.

How Reelhaus helps: The script writer builds the beat sheet and tracks each character’s arc across scenes.

03 · SHOTS

Put every shot on the board.

See the whole video as stills, scene by scene, and reorder it before anything renders.

How Reelhaus helps: Reel Board turns each line into a shot card with the framing and camera move written for you.

04 · CAST & LOOK

Same face. Same look. Every shot.

Nobody drifts between takes, and every shot keeps the same film look.

How Reelhaus helps: Character sheets lock the cast in four angles, and the look library keeps one style across the video.

05 · RENDER

See the price before you render.

Stop burning credits on rerolls. Check it on a still, then render the take you want.

How Reelhaus helps: Each shot shows its estimate on your own key, with zero markup. If a run fails on Reelhaus’s side, the credits come back.

06 · FIX

Say what to change.

“Tighten the pauses. Swap shot four.” Preview the change, then apply it.

How Reelhaus helps: The editor chat proposes the cut on the timeline, so you fix the take instead of starting over.
